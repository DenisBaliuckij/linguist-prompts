SOURCE RECORD
-------------
Name:          Universal Dependencies Russian-SynTagRus
URL:           https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-SynTagRus
Version:       UD v2.x (commit 26907c1, master branch)
License:       CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
Annotation:    CoNLL-U with FORM, LEMMA, UPOS, XPOS, FEATS, HEAD, DEPREL; human-corrected morphological and syntactic annotation
Note:          The treebank contains over 1,000,000 tokens (over 66,000 sentences) from contemporary fiction, popular science, newspaper and journal articles dated 1960-2016, and online news [citation:10]. It is the only human-corrected corpus of Russian with comprehensive morphological annotation including lemma, and a complete dependency tree for every sentence [citation:10]. Annotation was performed semi-automatically: first processed by the ETAP-3 parser, then manually corrected by linguists [citation:9].

The raw CoNLL-U files are hosted on Hugging Face [citation:7][citation:12]:
- ru_syntagrus-ud-train-a.conllu.gz (7.28 MB)
- ru_syntagrus-ud-train-b.conllu.gz (7.61 MB)
- ru_syntagrus-ud-train-c.conllu.gz (5.63 MB)
- ru_syntagrus-ud-dev.conllu.gz (2.6 MB)
- ru_syntagrus-ud-test.conllu.gz (2.66 MB)

These files were not retrievable as raw text in this session. The extraction below uses the verbatim form inventories and feature statistics present on the UD SynTagRus statistics page [citation:16], which lists non-trivial form–lemma pairs organized by morphological category. Every form–lemma pair quoted is verbatim from that source.
